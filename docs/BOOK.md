# The book — house style and image doctrine

Ported from harnessXray, where the plates were generated by the app itself and
Neo iterated hard on them. Two rules that look contradictory and are not.

## Two kinds of image, two opposite briefs

**Book plates** — a deliberate house style, held constant across every chapter,
because a book that changes its look per chapter reads as a collection of
clip-art. Neo art-directed these.

**In-app figures** — the opposite: specify content, never technique. From the
`infographic` skill:

> Do NOT dictate palettes, stroke widths, "flat vector", backgrounds or icon
> styles unless the user asked — an over-specified prompt comes back as
> clip-art. The model is a better designer than a checklist.

The escape clause ("unless the user asked") is exactly what the book plates use.

## The house style, read off the harnessXray plates

- **Ground**: near-black, flat, no gradient wash.
- **Medium**: chalk / soft pastel on blackboard. Visible tooth and texture;
  hand-drawn, not vector.
- **Palette**: four or five tones only — warm cream and pale tan for figures and
  lettering, cool blue-grey for structural edges and glass, near-black ground.
  No saturated colour anywhere.
- **Composition**: one literal metaphor per plate, centred, generous margins.
  A featureless human silhouette appears for scale and relatability.
- **Annotation**: numbers and short phrases hand-lettered _inside_ the drawing
  with a curved arrow to what they refer to ("40,000 tokens in", "200 back").
  The annotation is the teaching; the picture is the mnemonic.
- **Size**: 1536×1024, `quality: "high"`.

Each chapter carries **two** images: `NN-name.jpg` (the illustrative plate) and
`NN-name-tech.jpg` (the schematic of the same idea).

## Briefing rules that survive from the skill

- **Quoted strings render reliably; invented text does not.** Everything to be
  lettered goes in quotes, and labels stay short.
- One figure, one idea. Two ideas means two figures.
- A misspelled label → `edit_image` on the picture you just made, naming the
  correct spelling, rather than gambling on a fresh render.
- Never substitute a hand-authored SVG or HTML "poster". write_file is for text.
- Quantities do not become figures — write the numbers in a table.

## Quality bar

Neo's plates have no artifacts, no garbled lettering, no extra limbs. That is
not luck: it is regeneration until clean. Budget for several renders per plate
and reject anything with a flaw, however small — the book is the most-looked-at
surface in the app.
